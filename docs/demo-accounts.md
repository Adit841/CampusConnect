# Demo Accounts

Use these accounts to try CampusConnect as a student or a teacher. They are created
automatically the first time the backend starts, if they do not already exist.

The live site can take a minute or two to open on the first visit while the server wakes up.

**Live demo:** [campusconnect-z5v6.onrender.com](https://campusconnect-z5v6.onrender.com/)

## Logins

| Role | Email | Password | Extra ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student | `student.demo@campusconnect.edu` | `Demo@1234` | Enrollment `DEMO-STU-001` |
| Teacher | `teacher.demo@campusconnect.edu` | `Demo@1234` | Employee ID `DEMO-TCH-001` |

College for both accounts: **Arya College of Engineering & IT**.

## What each account is for

- **Student** — submit assignments, join clubs, register for events, chat, and post in the community.
- **Teacher** — manage subjects and assignments, grade submissions, and post announcements.

Admin cannot be created from the Register page. That role is provisioned on the server
with `ADMIN_PROVISION_EMAIL` and `ADMIN_PROVISION_PASSWORD`.
